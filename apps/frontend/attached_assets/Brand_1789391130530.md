# Orbit - Brand & Identity Guidelines

## 1. Brand Concept & Tone
- **Brand Name**: **Orbit**
- **Vibe / Identity**: Minimalist, sleek, modern, and high-contrast.
- **Design Principle**: Less is more. Consistency and clear hierarchy matter more than complex visuals. Let the content breathe.

## 2. Color Palette & Usage
A strict, high-contrast palette to keep the interface clean and focused.

| Role | Color Name | Hex Code | Usage Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Background** | True Black | `#000000` | Main app background, large surface areas, creating a deeply immersive dark mode feel. |
| **Primary Text/Action** | Pure White | `#FFFFFF` | Primary text (headers, body copy), primary buttons, and active states. Stands out sharply against the black background. |
| **Accent / Neutral** | Black Ash | `#2C2C2C` | Secondary backgrounds (cards, modals, dropdowns), borders, subtle dividers, and inactive states. Creates depth without introducing new colors. |

## 3. Typography
We use a single, versatile font family to maintain a unified look.

- **Primary Font Family**: **Inter** (A clean, highly legible sans-serif font optimized for UI).
- **Fallback Fonts**: `-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`

**Font Weights & Scale:**
- **Headings (H1, H2)**: `Bold (700)` or `Semi-Bold (600)` — Keep it heavy and impactful.
- **Body Text**: `Regular (400)` — Easy to read for long blocks of text.
- **Subtext / Metadata**: `Regular (400)` but smaller size — Use #2C2C2C or an opaque white (e.g., `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`) to push it back in the visual hierarchy.

## 4. UI Elements & Layout Rules
- **Buttons**:
  - **Primary**: White background (`#FFFFFF`) with Black text (`#000000`).
  - **Secondary**: Transparent background with White border (`#FFFFFF`) and White text (`#FFFFFF`).
- **Corners / Radii**: Pick one rule and stick to it. Either sharp corners (`0px`) for a brutalist edge, or slight rounding (`4px` to `8px`) for a softer modern look.
- **Spacing**: Use a consistent 8pt spacing system (8px, 16px, 24px, 32px, etc.) for padding and margins to keep everything aligned perfectly.

*Note: The goal is simplicity and consistency. Stick strictly to this minimal palette and typography across all project assets.*
